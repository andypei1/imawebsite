About

Institutional investment experience for Illinois students.

The academy provides a practical fund management environment where students develop the habits, judgment, and communication skills expected in investment roles.

What members do

Research, debate, and communicate investment recommendations.

Members research industries and companies, prepare valuation work, present investment cases, and participate in portfolio discussions with feedback from peers, directors, and alumni.
