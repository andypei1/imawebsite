About

Institutional investment experience for Illinois students.

The academy provides a practical fund management environment where students develop the habits, judgment, and communication skills expected in investment roles.

What members do

IMA members research industries and individual companies, build investment theses, prepare valuation work, present recommendations, and participate in portfolio discussions.

The program also emphasizes professional feedback from senior members, alumni, and directors, helping students sharpen their research process and communication.

Research

Students develop industry views and company-specific investment analysis.

Decision Making

Members connect valuation, catalysts, risks, and portfolio context.

Professional Standards

The academy is built around practical feedback and preparation for competitive roles.
