Eligibility

IMA recruitment is open to Gies students prepared for investing work.

Who can apply

Freshmen and sophomores in Gies College of Business are welcome to apply for the Fall 2026 recruitment cycle.

Recommended preparation

FIN 390 is recommended before applying. Candidates should be ready to discuss basic investing concepts and demonstrate preparation through the recruiting process.

IPO pathway

Investment Portfolio Organization, or IPO, offers a structured introduction to public-market investing and can help students build a foundation before pursuing IMA.
